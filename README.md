## 📌 Project Overview

BusinessPilot AI is a full-stack SaaS application that helps businesses manage their complete operations from a single dashboard.  

It includes multiple workspace modules such as:

- AI Assistant
- Sales Pipeline
- Customer Management
- Tasks & Appointments
- Invoicing
- Expense Tracking
- Analytics & Reports
- Team Management

This project was built independently to demonstrate real-world full-stack development and AI automation skills.

---

## ✨ Key Features

- AI Business Assistant (chat-based)
- Secure Authentication with Supabase
- Sales Pipeline & Lead Management
- Customer Relationship Management (CRM)
- Professional Invoicing system
- Analytics & CFO Insights
- Task and Appointment management
- Responsive modern dark UI
- SaaS Pricing plans
- Clean and scalable architecture
